Steak Recipes

Description

Steak Diane was a staple on menus in fine dining restaurants back in the day. It was prepared tableside where the server ignited the cognac for an impressive sight. This recipe is a much easier yet just as delicious version of the retro classic.

Ingredients

  • 2 (8 ounce) beef tenderloin steaks
  • 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon freshly ground black pepper
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1 tablespoon unsalted butter
  • 1/4 cup minced shallots
  • 1/4 cup sliced mushrooms
  • 1/4 cup brandy
  • 1/4 cup beef broth
  • 1/4 cup heavy cream
  • 1 teaspoon Dijon mustard
  • 1 teaspoon Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 tablespoon chopped fresh parsley
  • 1 tablespoon chopped fresh chives

Steps

  1. Season steaks with salt and pepper.
  2. Heat olive oil in a skillet over high heat. Add steaks and cook until browned, 2 to 3 minutes per side. Transfer to a plate.
  3. Reduce heat to medium and melt butter in the same skillet. Add shallots and mushrooms; cook and stir until softened, about 3 minutes.
  4. Remove skillet from heat and pour in brandy. Return skillet to heat and ignite brandy with a long lighter. Let flames die down and stir in beef broth, cream, mustard, and Worcestershire sauce. Bring to a simmer and cook until sauce is slightly reduced, about 5 minutes.
  5. Return steaks to skillet and cook until heated through, about 1 minute per side. Transfer steaks to a plate.
  6. Continue cooking sauce until thickened, about 5 minutes more. Pour sauce over steaks and sprinkle with parsley and chives.
