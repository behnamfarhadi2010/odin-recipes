Bacon Recipes

Description

These bacon-wrapped scallops are an easy appetizer to make with just three ingredients, and they taste extremely delicious!

Ingredients

  • 6 slices bacon
  • 12 sea scallops, rinsed and drained
  • 3 tablespoons fresh lemon juice
  • 1/4 cup chopped fresh parsley

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 450 degrees F (230 degrees C).
  2. Cut bacon slices in half.
  3. Wrap each scallop with a piece of bacon, and secure with a toothpick.
  4. Arrange wrapped scallops in a shallow baking dish.
  5. Drizzle lemon juice over scallops.
  6. Bake in preheated oven until scallops are opaque, 10 to 15 minutes.
  7. Remove from oven and let sit for 5 minutes before serving.
